# QA

Validate:

1. evidence and claims;
2. narration/display consistency;
3. audio/subtitle correspondence;
4. state timing at spoken anchors;
5. one dominant focus;
6. context persistence;
7. sufficient holds;
8. summary states;
9. typography antialiasing and weight;
10. restrained controls and consistent spacing;
11. missing assets/blank frames;
12. duration, codec, pixel format, frame rate, profile, level, fast start;
13. full decode test of final files.

Severity:

- High: incorrect claim, missing audio, broken video, blank frame, major sync error.
- Medium: unreadable text, insufficient hold, inconsistent focus, subtitle error.
- Low: minor spacing, subtle easing, noncritical decorative difference.
