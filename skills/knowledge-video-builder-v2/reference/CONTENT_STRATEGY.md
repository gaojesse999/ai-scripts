# Content Strategy

A content brief must define audience, viewer problem, promised outcome, thesis, hook, chapter order, concrete demo, visual transformations, duration, aspect ratio, exclusions, and claims that require qualification.

Prefer a narrative pattern such as:

1. hook/value;
2. what it is;
3. why it matters;
4. how it works;
5. concrete example;
6. limits or tradeoffs;
7. summary/action.

Do not use background history as the default opening.
